## Voice Rules
- **Always first person** — "We built Owen because we got tired of..." / "I've been there, staring at a trust account that didn't balance..."
- **Peer-to-peer frustrated but optimistic** — "God only knows what's buried in your AppFolio database, but here's how we fixed ours"
- **Specific war stories, not platitudes** — "Trust account that hadn't reconciled in 14 months" beats "data integrity issues"
- **Real numbers only** — "5-day month-end close" not "slow reporting", "owner distributions" not "improved cash management"
- **PM-specific terminology** — Trust accounting, CAM reconciliation, NOI by unit, management fees, owner draws, delinquency tracking, work orders, reserve contributions — speak like you live this
- **NEVER say**: "AI-powered", "revolutionary", "game-changing", "transform", "leverage", "cutting-edge", "disrupt"
- **Instead say**: "We built", "We fixed", "We automated", "We cleaned", "Here's what works"
- **Tone**: Peer-to-peer (PM CFO to PM CFO), frustrated-but-solving, unapologetic about the problem, confident about the fix
