## PRIMARY INDUSTRIES

### 1. Construction & Contracting

**Steve's edge:** Domain expert. Former/current construction CFO. Understands the workflows, pain points, software stack, and financial structure of construction companies.

**Why it matters for agents:**
- Construction companies are cash-rich and operations-poor
- They hate technology but desperately need it
- ERP/accounting systems are a mess (QB, Sage, BC, Excel hell)
- AR management, job costing, and forecasting are chronic pain points
- Nobody has built great AI tools for this space yet

**Target profiles:**
- General contractors ($5M–$150M revenue)
- Specialty subcontractors (MEP, concrete, framing)
- Construction CFOs and controllers
- Operations managers and project managers
- Owners of mid-market construction firms

**Monetizable problems agents should scan for:**
- ERP migration pain (Sage → BC, QB → Sage)
- AR aging and collections automation
- Job cost reporting inefficiency
- Subcontractor management overhead
- Bid/estimating workflow gaps
- Financial reporting for bonding and banking

### 2. HOA / Property Management

**Steve's edge:** Active pipeline. Running HOA discovery, enrichment, and outreach agents targeting HOA management companies and boards.

**Why it matters for agents:**
- HOAs are underserved by fintech
- Capital funding (special assessments, reserves) is a pain point
- HOA managers are overwhelmed with administrative work
- Data exists but is fragmented and messy

**Target profiles:**
- HOA management companies (small to mid-size)
- HOA board members (self-managed communities)
- Property management firms with HOA portfolios
- HOA financing and reserve study companies

**Monetizable problems:**
- Reserve fund shortfalls requiring capital
- Special assessment financing
- Vendor management automation
- Compliance and meeting documentation
